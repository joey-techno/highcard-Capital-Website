Term loan questions

Answered straight.

How fast is “fast,” honestly?

Options usually land within 24–48 hours of a complete application. Once you accept, funding can arrive in as little as 24 hours; most files fund within a few business days. Your advisor gives you a real calendar up front, not a slogan.

What does it cost to find out?

Nothing. Seeing your options is free, uses a soft credit pull, and carries no obligation. If you move forward, every cost is in writing before you sign. Lenders pay us at closing, which is why applying costs you zero.

Do I need collateral?

Not necessarily. Many term loans in our network are underwritten on revenue, not hard collateral. Where collateral strengthens your terms, your advisor will show you both versions and let you choose.

My credit took some hits. Waste of time?

No. Steady deposits and time in business carry more weight than the score for this product. If the numbers genuinely don’t work yet, your advisor will tell you why and what changes the answer, and that call is free too.

Term loan or line of credit?

Defined purchase → term loan. Recurring swings → line of credit. You don’t have to decide alone: one shared application covers both, and your advisor prices both paths.
