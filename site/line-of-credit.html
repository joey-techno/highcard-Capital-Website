Line of credit questions

Answered straight.

What do I pay if I never draw?

On most lines in our network: little to nothing. Interest only accrues on drawn funds. If a lender’s offer carries a maintenance or draw fee, it’s in your written cost sheet before you sign, never discovered after.

How fast can I get the line open?

Options usually arrive within 24–48 hours of a complete application, and lines often open within a few business days of acceptance. After that, draws are effectively instant.

Line of credit or term loan?

Recurring swings → line of credit. One defined purchase → term loan. Plenty of businesses run both: the loan buys the oven, the line smooths the flour bills. Your advisor will show you the split.

Will the line grow with us?

Usually, yes. Clean repayment history is the strongest argument for a bigger line, and your advisor can request increases as your revenue steps up, without restarting from zero.
