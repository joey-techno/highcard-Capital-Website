The broker question, head-on

Are you a lender? No, and that’s the point.

High Card Capital is a commercial-financing brokerage, not a direct lender. We arrange funding through a network of lending partners, and lenders pay us when your funding closes, which is why applying costs you nothing.

One bank can only offer you its own shelf. We shop the whole table, then argue your case at it. When a broker does the job right, you end up with terms a single lender was never going to volunteer.

  • You never pay to see optionsLenders compensate us at closing, disclosed in writing, every time.
  • Costs in writing before you signEvery dollar, on paper, before signatures. No discovery fees, ever.
  • Your data is never soldApplying here doesn’t put your phone on fifty call lists.
  • A named advisor owns your fileThe same voice from first call to funded, and after.
