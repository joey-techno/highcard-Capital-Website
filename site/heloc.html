What it is

Your house has been saving up for you.

A HELOC is a revolving credit line secured by the equity in your home: the gap between what it’s worth and what you owe. Business owners use it as the lowest-cost flexible capital available to them, especially in year one, when business credit is thin but personal equity is real.

Who it’s for

  • Founders funding a launch before the business has its own track record
  • Owners bridging a gap: a bulk inventory buy, a slow season, a closing date
  • Homeowners with meaningful equity who want the cheapest flexible line they can get

Plain talk: a HELOC is secured by your home. Your advisor will tell you when it’s the wrong tool: that candor is the service.

HELOC questions

Answered straight.

How fast can a HELOC close?

Three to four weeks is typical: appraisal and title work set the pace, not us. Need capital sooner? Many businesses take a fast term loan for the immediate need and open the HELOC behind it for the long game.

Is my house at risk?

The line is secured by your home: that’s what earns the low rate, and it’s a real obligation. We’ll never dress that up. Your advisor models the payment against your actual cash flow before you sign, and tells you plainly if the margin is too thin.

What does it cost to see my rate?

Nothing, and the look is a soft pull. If you proceed, closing costs and any fees appear in writing before signatures, the same no-surprises rule as every product on our table.

Why “See my rate” instead of “Apply”?

Because with property lending, the rate is the decision. We’d rather show you the real number first and let it argue for itself.
