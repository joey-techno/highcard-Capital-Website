You’ve got questions, we’ve got answers.

How does a loan with High Card work?

Complete a quick online application to determine your business’s eligibility, or do it over the phone. Once it’s filled out, your estimated loan terms can be customized by adjusting the amount and duration. After you select the loan that fits and the application is complete, you’re prompted to electronically sign a contract emailed to you, and the funds are wired into your account shortly thereafter.

I’m already indebted to another company. Can you still help me?

Usually, yes. Depending on the numbers, that’s either a consolidation that replaces expensive daily-payment debt with something saner, or a second position layered carefully on top of what exists. Bring your current statements and your advisor will map the exits with you.

What factors are you taking into account?

Many data points go into your rate and terms: time in business, monthly revenue, the health and consistency of your deposits, and credit history among them. Revenue and steady cash flow carry the most weight, so a less-than-perfect score rarely rules you out.

What interest amount am I paying?

There are various products we work with, and APRs will vary from 12% and up. Many data points go into the rates and terms, but all of it is presented transparently before any agreements are drawn up.

How much money will my business qualify for?

Every business is different, but typically we’re able to fund approximately 10% of your annual revenue. Of course, that’s an oversimplified answer, and the best way to determine how much you’d receive is to apply.
