Legal

Privacy policy.

How High Card Capital collects, uses, shares, and protects your information when you use our website and services. In plain language, because you deserve to actually understand it.

Last updated July 21, 2026.

What we collect.

Information you give us

Your name, email, phone number, business details, and anything else you provide directly, including through a form or a text message. When you apply for funding, this can include the financial information needed to match you with lenders, such as bank statements and revenue details.

Information collected automatically

Technical data such as your IP address, browser, device details, and how you use our website, including how you interact with our text messaging.

How we use your data.

To deliver our services

To provide, operate, and improve what we offer, including our text messaging and the process of matching you with lending partners.

To communicate with you

To answer your questions, including those sent by text, and, if you opt in, to send you marketing.

To understand and improve, and to meet our obligations

To see how our website and text features are used so we can make them better, and to comply with the law and enforce our terms.

Sharing & your rights.

We do not sell your data

Your information, including anything you share by text, is never sold. We share it only when we are legally required to, or when it is necessary to provide a service you have asked for, such as arranging financing through our lending partners. When we do share information with a partner, we take steps to see that they handle it securely and in line with this policy.

Text messaging and consent

If you provide your phone number and consent to receive text messages, we may text you about your application, your account, or, where you have opted in, marketing. Message and data rates may apply. You can opt out of marketing texts at any time by replying STOP, and you can reply HELP for assistance. Opting out of marketing does not stop the transactional texts needed to service an active application.

Your choices

You can ask us what personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. We honor these requests except where we are obligated to keep certain information for legal, administrative, or security reasons. To make a request, use the contact details below.

How we protect it.

Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your information against unauthorized access or misuse. No internet-based system is ever completely secure, so while we work hard to safeguard your data, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Cookies and similar technology

We may use cookies and similar technologies to improve your experience, run analytics, and personalize what you see. You can manage or disable cookies through your browser settings, though some parts of the site may not work as well if you do.

Links to other sites

Our website may link to sites we do not control. We are not responsible for their privacy practices, so please review their policies before sharing anything with them.

Children’s privacy

Our services, including text messaging, are not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will remove it.

Updates & contact.

Updates to this policy

This policy may change over time. When it does, we will revise the “Last updated” date at the top of the page. Continuing to use our services after a change means you accept the updated policy.

Questions?

If you have questions about this policy or how we handle your information, including anything related to text messaging, contact us at hello@highcardcapital.example or through our contact page.
