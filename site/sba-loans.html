What it is

Patient capital, guaranteed by Uncle Sam.

The U.S. Small Business Administration doesn’t lend directly: it guarantees a large slice of your loan, which lets lenders offer rates and terms they’d never touch otherwise. 7(a) covers most uses; 504 handles owner-occupied real estate and heavy equipment.

Who it’s for

  • Established businesses (two-plus years) making a big, planned move
  • Acquisitions, expansions, real estate, and refinancing expensive debt

SBA questions

Answered straight.

How long does SBA really take?

Two to ten weeks depending on the program, the lender, and how fast documents come together. SBA Express programs move quickest; real estate deals take longest. We work exclusively with lenders who close, and you’ll have a realistic calendar after your first advisor call, not a fantasy one.

Do I qualify?

The strongest SBA files show two-plus years in business, solid revenue, and credit in the high 600s or better. Not there yet? Your advisor will say so plainly, and usually has a bridge: many businesses take a term loan now and refinance into SBA later.

What can I use it for?

Working capital, equipment, inventory, acquisitions, partner buyouts, owner-occupied real estate, and refinancing expensive debt. What it can’t do: passive investments and speculation.

Why go through a broker instead of my bank?

Your bank has one SBA shelf and one credit box. We package your file once and put it in front of the SBA lenders most likely to approve your industry and size: the difference between asking one teller and working the whole table.

What does your help cost me?

Seeing your options costs nothing. If your loan closes, we’re compensated within the SBA’s regulated limits and every dollar of cost appears in writing before you sign. No surprises is the whole brand.
