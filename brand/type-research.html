C · Icon direction

The documented convergence across Stripe, Wise, Square, and the SMB-lender field — validated by your favorite, Fundbox.

The spec: line icons, 2px stroke on a 24px grid (2.5px at hero sizes — the AmEx "prestige" weight), rounded caps, contained in 48–64px cream tiles. Base = Lucide (1,500+ icons, adjustable stroke), gaps filled from Tabler, plus 3–5 Phosphor-duotone hero pictograms with sage fills. Ink strokes by default; Racing Green only on hover/active — so green always means "interactive." Chevrons throughout get customized to the logo's peak angle.
