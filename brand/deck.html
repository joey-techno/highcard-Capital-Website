01 · The Idea

One shape, three meanings.

The mark is a single upright card holding a peak — an A with no crossbar. Every read works in our favor, and none of them is a gimmick.

The Ace

The peak inside the card is the ace — the high card. It's the name of the company drawn as a single glyph.

The Climb

Read again and it's an arrow pointing up: growth, funding, momentum for the businesses we back.

The Summit

And it's a peak — "High." Quiet altitude, not casino flash. Premium without the gold leaf.

03 · Color

Racing Green & cream, disciplined.

British Racing Green (#004225) leads; cream is the warmth; the neutrals do the everyday work. Every dark surface in the brand is one single flat shade — Table Green #00331D — no gradients, no texture, no light-and-dark. No gold anywhere.

04 · Typography — chosen

Poppins & Inter — the researched system.

Locked after live research across banks, lenders, and fintech: Poppins for display (the logo's own family — and notably, no major bank uses it, so it's ours), Inter for body and UI (the fintech gold standard), and tabular figures on every number. The signature stays: every headline ends with a period in Racing Green.

05 · Iconography

Seventeen icons, one hand.

Custom-drawn on the same grid as the logo — identical stroke weight, identical rounded terminals. These cover every section of the site: the four products, the value props, and the UI. Delivered as individual SVGs in brand/icons/.
