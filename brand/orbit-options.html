The orb light, two layouts.

Same racing green energy, new light: a small glowing globe with a soft halo and a short trail, gliding along faint arcs. No more streaks. Two compositions to choose between, both with the soft cursor depth shift, both quiet enough to never fight the headline.

Layout 1The Orbitsfour concentric arcs curve around the proof card like orbital paths, orbs gliding along them at different speeds, a few faint stars in the distance
Layout 2The Horizongentle arcs span the full width low across the hero like the curve of a globe, orbs drifting along the horizon beneath your message

Designer note: Layout 1 gives the hero a quiet center of gravity at the proof card, everything orbits the money. Layout 2 is even calmer and more spacious, the world under your message. Both can run a touch brighter or dimmer once one is on the real page.
