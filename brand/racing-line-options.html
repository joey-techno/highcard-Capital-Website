Three finished takes on the direction you picked. Same rules as always: flat Table Green, cream and sage light only, motion off for reduced-motion visitors. All three have the upgraded light (glowing tapered pulse with a bright head) and a soft depth shift when you move your cursor. The differences are in composition.

V1The Apexa slipstream of lines carves one deliberate corner between your headline and the proof card, then exits behind it. The eye follows the line from message to money.
V2The Slipstreama tight pack of nine lines streams low across the hero like a track surface, light pulsing through at staggered speeds. The top stays clean so the headline owns the air.
V3The Circuityour card frame becomes the track: a double keyline ring, cropped behind the proof card, with light lapping it continuously and sage ticks at the apexes.

My take as your designer: V1 is the strongest. It has one clear compositional idea, it physically connects the headline to the funded card, and it scales cleanly at every screen size. V2 is the calmest, V3 is the most branded but competes a little with the proof card for attention.
