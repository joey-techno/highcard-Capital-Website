## FAQ ("Asked by merchants. Answered straight.") — UPGRADED SET
Proven high-intent questions (researched against Bluevine / Fundbox / Lendio
patterns — each one removes a specific objection):

1. How fast can I actually get funded? — speed, the #1 question; give the
   real range per product (24–48h term/LOC, SBA measured in weeks, honestly).
2. Will checking my options hurt my credit? — the soft-pull answer: "No —
   seeing your options is a soft pull. A hard pull only happens if you
   accept an offer, and we tell you before it does."
3. Do I qualify? — plain minimums: time in business, monthly revenue,
   credit range. Letting people self-qualify REDUCES junk leads.
4. What documents do I need? — "3–6 months of bank statements and a
   two-minute application" for most products; SBA/HELOC list their extras.
5. What does it cost to work with you? — the broker-fee candor answer: who
   pays us, when, and "your costs are in writing before you sign. No fee to
   see options."
6. My credit isn't perfect. Is this a waste of time? — keep; it's the
   empathy question. Answer with what matters more than the score
   (revenue, time in business).
7. I already have an advance or loan. Can you still help? — keep;
   consolidation/second-position is a huge SMB segment.
8. Which product is right for me — Term Loan, SBA, HELOC, or Line of
   Credit? — link the comparison table + "or ask your advisor."
9. What happens after I apply? — the process demystified: "A real advisor
   calls you same-day, options within 24–48 hours, you choose, funds wire."
   Kills the fear of spam-call hell — pair with "your data is never sold."
10. Are you a lender or a broker? — the trust question answered head-on
    with the candor block: brokerage, network of lenders, why that gets
    you better terms than one bank's shelf.
Side card: "Something else on your mind? → Ask us directly"
