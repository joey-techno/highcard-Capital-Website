Small light, big room.

Four quiet luminous accents, researched from the sites that do this best. Each one is a single small light in one place, the message keeps the whole stage. These are sage and cream light on the flat Table Green, not surface gradients: the green itself never changes shade. All pure CSS, feather light, and they hold still for reduced-motion visitors.

Accent 1The Bloomone distant sage light behind the proof card, breathing slowly. The money glows, quietly.
Accent 2The Eclipsethe rim of a huge unseen circle catches light just past the top right corner, like a planet edge at dawn. The most otherworldly, and you barely notice it at first.
Accent 3The Light Leakwarm light spills softly from above the fold onto the headline, completely still. The subtlest of the four.
Accent 4The Threada single narrow ribbon of aurora drifting high in the top right, extremely slow. One small piece of sky.

Designer note: the research ranked rim light first for restraint and I agree, The Eclipse is the one that makes people lean in without ever shouting. The Bloom is the warmest. The Light Leak is the safest. The Thread has the most personality. Any of them can be tuned brighter or dimmer on the real page.
